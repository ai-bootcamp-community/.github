## 🧠 About AI BOOTCAMP

AI BOOTCAMP is a Czech community-driven event bringing together people who build, experiment with, and implement artificial intelligence in the real world.

No corporate buzzwords. No sales pitches. Just passionate people sharing their knowledge, real-world experiences, experiments, and lessons learned.

Inspired by the Barcamp philosophy, we believe that **the best way to learn is to share what you build.**

## ⚡ What we're passionate about

- **AI Agents & Agentic Workflows** — Building autonomous systems and complex AI workflows.
- **LLMs & RAG** — Retrieval, embeddings, fine-tuning, model evaluation, and optimization.
- **AI Engineering** — MCP, tool calling, observability, tracing, and infrastructure.
- **Automation & Integration** — Bringing AI into real-world products and business processes.
- **Open Source & Experimentation** — Sharing tools, code, research, and practical discoveries.

## 🤝 Our GitHub Organization

This GitHub organization is a place for the AI BOOTCAMP community to share open-source projects, experiments, useful tools, and resources.

Whether it's a prototype, an AI agent, an automation workflow, or something completely experimental — we believe great ideas deserve to be shared.
